# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-21*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Hex is a UK job board that connects employers with qualified candidates across all 20 sectors — free for job seekers, subscription-based for employers.
**What it does:** Hex is a two-sided recruitment marketplace. Job seekers create profiles, upload CVs, browse jobs, and apply for free. Employers subscribe to post jobs, search candidate profiles, manage applications through a pipeline, schedule interviews, make offers, and track hiring analytics — all in one platform.
**Product category:** Online recruitment platform / Job board
**Product type:** SaaS marketplace (two-sided)
**Business model:** Freemium. Job seekers: free forever. Employers: 14-day free trial (no card required), then £29.99/mo (Standard, 3 jobs) or £59.99/mo (Professional, unlimited jobs + analytics + priority access). Payments via Stripe, monthly billing, 7-day cancellation notice.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** UK SMEs and mid-size companies (10–500 employees) across all 20 sectors. From independent restaurants and care homes to hotel groups, retail chains, and regional service companies. Not targeting enterprise (500+), but anything up to mid-size is fair game.
**Decision-makers:** Business owners, hiring managers, HR leads, operations managers. In smaller companies it's often the owner wearing multiple hats; in mid-size companies it's the HR lead or ops manager.
**Primary use case:** Employer needs to fill a vacancy quickly with a qualified candidate, without paying per-hire agency fees.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "I need to post a job and get qualified applicants within days, not weeks"
- "I need to find candidates with the right skills in my area without trawling LinkedIn"
- "I need one place to manage applications, interviews, offers, and onboarding"
**Use cases:**
- Restaurant owner hiring a head chef — posts job, reviews applications, schedules interview, makes offer, all in-platform
- Care home manager filling multiple nursing roles — uses candidate search + filters to proactively reach out
- Job seeker relocating to a new city — sets up job alerts by sector + location, applies directly from mobile

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** UK employers — especially SMEs outside London — struggle to find qualified candidates without expensive recruitment agencies or sifting through generic mass-market job boards.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Indeed/Reed/Jobtoday: High volume, low signal. CV black holes — candidates apply and never hear back, employers drown in irrelevant applications. No candidate search or pipeline. Per-listing costs add up.
- Recruitment agencies: 15-25% of salary fee. Overkill for a single hire. Slow turnaround. Less control.
- LinkedIn: Expensive recruiter seats. Passive candidates. Not built for high-turnover sectors like hospitality/healthcare.
- Caterer.com/Chef's Pencil: Hospitality-only. No cross-sector coverage. No pipeline management. Dated UX.
**What it costs them:** Weeks of unfilled vacancies, overtime costs, lost revenue from understaffing, manager time spent screening.
**Emotional tension:** "I can't afford another bad hire" / "I'm spending all my time recruiting instead of running the business" / "I just need someone who'll actually show up"

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Indeed, Reed, Jobtoday, LinkedIn — mass-market UK job boards. Fall short on: CV black holes (candidates apply and never hear back), no built-in pipeline or interview scheduling, per-listing or per-seat pricing, no direct candidate messaging.
**Secondary:** Caterer.com, Chef's Pencil — hospitality-specific boards. Limited to one sector, no cross-sector coverage, often outdated UX, no pipeline management.
**Indirect:** Recruitment agencies (Adecco, Hays, local agencies) — 15-25% of salary per hire, slower turnaround, less employer control over the process.
**Positioning angle:** Hex is faster, cheaper, and less friction than all of them — no agency fees, no CV black holes, one flat subscription for the full toolkit.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- All-in-one platform: post jobs + search candidates + manage pipeline + interview scheduling + offers + analytics in one tool
- Free for candidates (drives candidate supply)
- £29.99/mo flat subscription vs per-listing or per-hire fees
- UK-focused with all 20 sectors covered (not just tech or hospitality)
- Built-in messaging, interview scheduling with conflict detection, and offer management
**How we do it differently:** One subscription gives employers the full recruitment toolkit — no add-on fees for candidate search, messaging, or analytics. Candidates never pay, so the talent pool grows organically.
**Why that's better:** Predictable cost for employers. Better candidate experience drives more applications. Employers manage the entire hire lifecycle without switching tools.
**Why customers choose us:** "I can post a job, message candidates, schedule interviews, and make an offer — all from one dashboard for less than the cost of a single agency placement."

|-----------|----------|
| "I already use Indeed/Reed" | Hex gives you candidate search, pipeline management, interview scheduling, and analytics that Indeed doesn't — for a flat monthly fee instead of per-listing costs. |
| "Will I get enough candidates?" | 1,000+ candidates already registered across 20 sectors, and growing. Free access for job seekers means the pool grows faster than paid boards. |
| "I'm not sure it's worth paying for" | The first 100 employers get 12 months completely free — no card, no catch. Use it for a year, see the results, then decide. Spots are limited. |

**Anti-persona:** Enterprise companies (500+ employees) who need ATS integrations, bulk hiring workflows, or global coverage. Hex is built for SMEs and mid-size companies making 1-50 hires at a time.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustrated with high agency fees. Tired of irrelevant applications on mass boards. No visibility into what's working.
**Pull:** 12 months free for first 100 employers — zero risk. All-in-one platform. UK sector coverage. No agency fees ever.
**Habit:** "We've always used Indeed" / "Our agency knows our business" / "Posting on Facebook works well enough"
**Anxiety:** "Will candidates actually be on a newer platform?" / "What happens after the 12 months?" / "Is it secure?"

## Goals
**Business goal:** Fill 100 free employer slots as fast as possible. First 100 employers get 12 months free access (full platform, no card required). After the 100 cap is reached, new employers pay from signup. Candidates are always free.
**Conversion action:** Employer signs up for free launch offer → posts first job → becomes an active user during the 12-month window → converts to paid when the free period ends.
**Primary metric:** Speed to 100 employer sign-ups. Secondary: candidate registrations (target 1,000+), jobs posted per employer, applications per job.
**Launch model:** Free launch offer creates urgency (limited to 100 slots) and removes all friction for early adopters. The 12-month runway gives employers time to see value before paying, building retention and word-of-mouth.
