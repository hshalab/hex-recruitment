# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-04-11*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Thrive is a UK job board that connects employers with qualified candidates across all 20 sectors — free for job seekers, subscription-based for employers.
**What it does:** Thrive is a two-sided recruitment marketplace. Job seekers create profiles, upload CVs, browse jobs, and apply for free. Employers subscribe to post jobs, search candidate profiles, manage applications through a pipeline, schedule interviews (with Google Calendar sync), add pre-screening questions, make offers, and track hiring analytics — all in one platform.
**Product category:** Online recruitment platform / Job board
**Product type:** SaaS marketplace (two-sided)
**Business model:** Freemium. Job seekers: free forever. Employers: 3 months free for the first 600 (no card required), then £99/mo (Standard — single plan with unlimited jobs, candidate search, messaging, pipeline, interview scheduling, and analytics). Payments via Stripe, monthly billing, 7-day cancellation notice.
**Domain:** thrivecareer.co.uk

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** UK SMEs and mid-size companies (10–500 employees) across all 20 sectors. From independent restaurants and care homes to hotel groups, retail chains, tech startups, and regional service companies. Not targeting enterprise (500+), but anything up to mid-size is fair game.
**Decision-makers:** Business owners, hiring managers, HR leads, operations managers. In smaller companies it's often the owner wearing multiple hats; in mid-size companies it's the HR lead or ops manager.
**Primary use case:** Employer needs to fill a vacancy quickly with a qualified candidate, without paying per-hire agency fees.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "I need to post a job and get qualified applicants within days, not weeks"
- "I need to find candidates with the right skills in my area without trawling LinkedIn"
- "I need one place to manage applications, interviews, offers, and onboarding"
**Use cases:**
- Restaurant owner hiring a head chef — posts job with pre-screening questions, reviews applications, schedules interview (syncs to Google Calendar), makes offer, all in-platform
- Tech startup filling a developer role — uses candidate search + filters to proactively reach out
- Job seeker relocating to a new city — sets up job alerts by sector + location, applies directly from mobile

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** UK employers — especially SMEs outside London — struggle to find qualified candidates without expensive recruitment agencies or sifting through generic mass-market job boards.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Indeed/Reed/Jobtoday: High volume, low signal. CV black holes — candidates apply and never hear back, employers drown in irrelevant applications. No candidate search or pipeline. Per-listing costs add up.
- Recruitment agencies: 15-25% of salary fee. Overkill for a single hire. Slow turnaround. Less control.
- LinkedIn: Expensive recruiter seats. Passive candidates. Not built for high-turnover sectors.
- Sector-specific boards: Limited to one sector. No cross-sector coverage. No pipeline management. Dated UX.
**What it costs them:** Weeks of unfilled vacancies, overtime costs, lost revenue from understaffing, manager time spent screening.
**Emotional tension:** "I can't afford another bad hire" / "I'm spending all my time recruiting instead of running the business" / "I just need someone who'll actually show up"

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Indeed, Reed, Jobtoday, LinkedIn — mass-market UK job boards. Fall short on: CV black holes (candidates apply and never hear back), no built-in pipeline or interview scheduling, per-listing or per-seat pricing, no direct candidate messaging.
**Secondary:** Sector-specific boards (Caterer.com, etc.) — limited to one sector, no cross-sector coverage, often outdated UX, no pipeline management.
**Indirect:** Recruitment agencies (Adecco, Hays, local agencies) — 15-25% of salary per hire, slower turnaround, less employer control over the process.
**Positioning angle:** Thrive is faster, cheaper, and less friction than all of them — no agency fees, no CV black holes, one flat subscription for the full toolkit.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- All-in-one platform: post jobs + search candidates + manage pipeline + interview scheduling (Google Calendar sync) + pre-screening questions + offers + analytics in one tool
- Company profile auto-fill from website (Firecrawl + AI extraction)
- Pre-interview interest check — candidates confirm interest before scheduling
- Sign in with Google (alongside email/password)
- Free for candidates (drives candidate supply)
- £99/mo flat subscription vs per-listing or per-hire fees
- UK-focused with all 20 sectors covered (not just tech or one sector)
- Built-in messaging, interview scheduling with conflict detection, calendar sync, and offer management
**How we do it differently:** One subscription gives employers the full recruitment toolkit — no add-on fees for candidate search, messaging, or analytics. Candidates never pay, so the talent pool grows organically.
**Why that's better:** Predictable cost for employers. Better candidate experience drives more applications. Employers manage the entire hire lifecycle without switching tools.
**Why customers choose us:** "I can post a job, message candidates, schedule interviews, and make an offer — all from one dashboard for less than the cost of a single agency placement."

## Switching Dynamics

**Pull:** 3 months free for first 600 employers — zero risk. All-in-one platform. UK sector coverage. No agency fees ever.
**Habit:** "We've always used Indeed" / "Our agency knows our business" / "Posting on Facebook works well enough"
**Anxiety:** "Will candidates actually be on a newer platform?" / "What happens after the 3 months?" / "Is it secure?"

## Goals
**Business goal:** Fill 600 free employer slots as fast as possible. First 600 employers get 3 months free access (full platform, no card required). After the 600 cap is reached, new employers pay from signup. Candidates are always free.
**Conversion action:** Employer signs up for free launch offer → posts first job → becomes an active user during the 3-month window → converts to paid when the free period ends.
**Primary metric:** Speed to 600 employer sign-ups. Secondary: candidate registrations, jobs posted per employer, applications per job.
**Launch model:** Free launch offer ("3 for 600") creates urgency (limited to 600 slots) and removes all friction for early adopters. The 3-month runway gives employers time to see value before paying, building retention and word-of-mouth.
